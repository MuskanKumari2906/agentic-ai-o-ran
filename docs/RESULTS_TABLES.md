- Reference comparisons: ns3_default (5G-LENA defaults: antenna arrays with beamforming, fast fading) and ns3_closest (same, UEs attached to the closest gNB). Median per-UE SINR: twin 3.9 dB, ns-3 4.6 dB and 5.6 dB.
- Upper tail: ns-3 reaches higher SINR (p90 25-27 dB vs 16 dB) because its antenna arrays add beamforming gain that the twin's single sector element does not model.
- Lower tail: about 20% of ns-3 UEs are below -15 dB in every ns-3 configuration, including attach-to-closest; the twin has no such tail. The cause (beam selection under full-buffer interference, or how the example records SINR) was not resolved within this build and is listed as an open item.
- ns3_matched (1x1 array, omnidirectional beam) removes the sector directivity the twin models; it is not like-for-like and is shown only for transparency.
- Samples: ns-3 195 UEs with SINR reports (of 285 dropped); twin 2850 UEs. ns-3.48 + 5G-LENA v5.1, cttc-nr-3gpp-calibration-user.
